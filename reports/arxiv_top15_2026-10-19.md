## Method & caveats

- Paper list from the arXiv API (`submittedDate` window over the categories above, cross-listings deduplicated).
- Reference lists and citation counts from the Semantic Scholar Graph API at report time.
- Papers submitted in the last few days may not be indexed by Semantic Scholar yet, or their bibliographies may not be parsed yet; such papers cannot be ranked and are only reflected in the coverage numbers above.
- References that Semantic Scholar cannot match to an indexed work count as 0 in the weighted score.
- Rankings cover the 2032 papers with Semantic Scholar data.
